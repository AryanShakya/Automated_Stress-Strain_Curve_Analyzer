Description
The tensile test allows to engineers and researchers to know the mechanical properties of the engineering materials. In this dataset is showed the results for the tensile test of the two dog bones specimens.  The specimen material is AISI 1015 steel. Tesnile test test was realized in the MTS-810 testing machine.  Some properties like yield limit, ultimate stress and hardening coefficient are obtained.
